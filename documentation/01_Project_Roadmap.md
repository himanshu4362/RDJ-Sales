# Long-Term Vision

The RDJ platform is designed to evolve into a complete Business Operating System capable of supporting:

- Customer Experience
- Sales
- Service & Repairs
- Payments
- CRM
- Dealer Management
- Inventory
- Staff Management
- Communication
- Analytics
- Future mobile applications

without requiring architectural rewrites.
